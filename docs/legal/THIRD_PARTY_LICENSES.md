## Rules used by the matrix

**P (permissive code):** MIT OR Apache-2.0 at the user's option, except specifically identified files. Source modification, browser/WASM binary distribution and commercial distribution appear permitted when applicable license conditions are met. MIT requires copyright and permission text. Apache-2.0 additionally requires license copy, change notices, preservation of relevant notices and NOTICE attribution where supplied; contains contributor patent grant and patent-litigation termination. MIT has no equivalent express patent grant. Neither grants trademark rights or clears third-party codec patents. Choosing MIT for dual-licensed code does not relicense Apache-only files or other dependencies. For this project retain upstream NOTICE and both license texts when integrating to avoid losing attribution context.

**Assets:** asset/font licenses apply separately to source and binary distribution. OFL fonts require copyright/OFL notices, respect Reserved Font Names and font-specific restrictions; their inclusion does not automatically relicense application code. Icons may require ISC and MIT attribution. All ArtCraft marks and app icons/branding are excluded from Astralys, regardless of any limited upstream permission. No Adobe branding is copied.

## Distribution boundary

The shipped browser app uses original code and browser-provided APIs; it does not redistribute browser codec implementations. Original app code is MIT. No bundled fonts/icons/photos: system fonts and original CSS/inline markup only. Development packages and their complete lockfile inventory are recorded separately after installation. Rust spike dependencies are isolated; no Rust/WASM distribution is approved until its resolved licenses and notices are recorded. The audit does not establish trademark clearance, clean-room provenance, third-party patents or a security certification.
